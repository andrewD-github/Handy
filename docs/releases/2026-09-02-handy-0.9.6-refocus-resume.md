## Known accepted limitation

Chromium/WebView can reuse one native focused control after a prompt is submitted and cleared. With refocus resume enabled, returning to that reused native target may append pending text to the new prompt. The setting remains explicit and defaults off in code; this installation enables it because that tradeoff was accepted for this workflow.
